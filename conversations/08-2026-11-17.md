**The controller:** this is probably what's known as "stick drift". It usually comes from dust or worn parts in the thumbstick, so seeing it in other apps fits. Try these in order:

1. **Clean it without opening it.** Take the battery out. Tilt the stick hard to one side and blow compressed air into the gap at its base, all the way round. If that's not enough, put a drop of 90%+ isopropyl alcohol at the base while rolling the stick in full circles and pressing it in. Let it dry completely before putting the battery back. This often fixes dust-caused drift, at least for a while.
2. **Warranty:** Meta's is 1 year, so your Quest 2 is probably out of it. If it's newer than that, ask them for a replacement first.
3. **Replace the thumbstick:** if cleaning doesn't last, the part itself is worn, and opening it just to clean won't help. Replacement thumbstick parts for Quest 2 controllers are cheap, and iFixit has a step-by-step guide. As far as I know the stick plugs in on a small cable, so no soldering is needed, but the face of the controller is glued down and has to be pried off carefully. Check the guide before buying anything.
4. **Or buy another controller:** a used or new left controller on its own costs more but is the least effort.

## Claude

Bowling is on the shelf as "Bowling" (icon: the ball and one pin). It passes its emulator test, but nothing has been tried on the Quest yet, and it is not committed.

**Putting the pins back:** I made the lane run itself, like a real one, so there is no gesture at all:
- A roll ends when everything has come to rest (or after 10 s), and the ball floats back to where it started.
- If pins fell, their number rises over the rack ("7", "Strike", "Spare") and the fallen ones are taken away, so you can go for the spare.
- After the second counted roll, or when none is left standing, all ten set themselves up again.
- A roll that knocks nothing down is not counted.

I avoided a trigger for this because the cube's trigger kept going off while grabbing. If you would rather reset at will, say so.

**What you get:**
- **Ball:** purple and glossy, real size, with two finger holes and a thumb hole. It floats where you let the icon go. Closing a hand anywhere on it slides it into the hold of `grip_hold`, mirrored for the left hand.
- **Pins:** ten, real size, white with two red collars, racked on the floor about 5 m ahead, nearer if the room is too short. They can be picked up and thrown too.
- **Physics:** the ball flies with the hand's speed times 1.25 (my guess, to be tuned by feel). A held ball or pin also knocks what it touches.
- **Sound:** a woody knock for pins and a thump for the ball landing, both made in code.

**Things to check on the Quest:**
- **The grip:** I fitted it by script against the game hand's grab pose: palm on the surface, curled fingers at the finger holes, only about 7 mm in. The hand is under `grip_hold` in `resources/bowling/bowling.blend` so you can place it yourself.
- **Re-exporting:** after editing the `.blend`, run `scripts/bowling/export-bowling.py`; `build-bowling.py` overwrites the file.
- **Reflections:** the room has no environment map, so the ball gets a small one of its own, generated when the gadget is first summoned. That worked in the emulator; watch for a hitch on the headset.
- **Frame rate:** ten pins tumbling at once is the heaviest physics so far.

The code is in `src/bowling/bowling.js` and the test is `tests/bowling.mjs`.
